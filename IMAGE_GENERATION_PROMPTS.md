## 📋 SZCZEGÓŁY TECHNICZNE

**Standardowe ustawienia wszystkich promptów Flux 1.0:**
- Aspect Ratio: 3:2 (1200×800px)
- Quality: max (maksymalna jakość)
- Style: "photojournalism, documentary, professional service photography"
- Lighting: Natural + professional work lighting, soft shadows
- Camera: Canon 5D Mark IV / Sony A7R IV aesthetic (documentary photography standard)
- Color Grading: Natural, professional, warm indoor lighting, cool shadows
- Texture: Slight film grain, natural artifacts from real photography
- Composition: Documentary framing, real service execution moments

**ZAKAZANE elementy:**
- ❌ Watermarks
- ❌ AI-look plasticity
- ❌ Stock photo poses
- ❌ Over-sharpness
- ❌ Unnatural colors
- ❌ Logo branding
- ❌ Generic stock sets
- ❌ Perfect unrealistic quality

**WYMAGANE elementy:**
- ✅ Real service moments captured
- ✅ Professional but authentic atmosphere
- ✅ Dust, light rays, real environmental details
- ✅ Natural imperfections (slight motion blur okay, atmospheric depth required)
- ✅ LOCAL POLISH CONTEXT (architecture, interior styles)
- ✅ Technical precision (equipment, hands, execution)
- ✅ Documentary photography aesthetic

## 🔄 WORKFLOW GENERACJI

**Flux 1.0 API / CLI Generate:**
```bash
# Każdy prompt generuj indywidualnie:
flux generate --prompt="[prompt text]" --output="filename.jpg" --quality=max --aspect=3:2
```

**Po generacji:**
1. Optymalizacja JPG (mozjpeg lub ImageMagick):
   ```bash
   convert input.jpg -quality 82 -interlace Plane output.jpg
   ```
2. Weryfikacja rozdzielczości: `identify output.jpg`
3. Umieszczenie w `/assets/images/`
4. Integracja w HTML
